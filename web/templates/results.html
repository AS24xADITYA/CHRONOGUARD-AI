Model Scope & Architectural Boundaries:

1. Predictive Forecaster, Not RL Simulator: ChronoGuard models P(state_{t+k} | history) to detect temporal attack build-up; it does not simulate hypothetical defender actions or autonomous counter-measures.
2. Fixed Count-Based Windows: Traffic is aggregated into fixed chunks of W=10 consecutive flows rather than variable wall-clock windows, stabilizing against sudden packet bursts while approximating session continuity.
3. Metadata-Only (No DPI): Inferences are derived solely from flow-level velocity, packet length dispersion, and TCP control flag transitions, remaining fully effective on encrypted (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) telemetry without payload inspection.
